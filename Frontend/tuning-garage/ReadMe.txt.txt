
# Tuning Garage

This directory exists off the main road.

**Tuning Garage** is where React-based projects and internship assignments live — work that focuses on adjustment, refinement, and real-world constraints rather than exploration of fundamentals.

These projects are not chapters in the main journey.  
They are practical runs.  
Exercises in speed, structure, and correctness.

Here, the focus shifts from *learning what is possible* to *delivering what is required*.

---

## What Lives Here

- React project assignments  
- Internship tasks and deliverables  
- Component-driven implementations  
- Real-world layout, logic, and state challenges  
- Code written under constraints and expectations  

This is where theory meets timelines.

---

## How to Read This Directory

- Each folder represents a **single assignment or project**
- Naming is kept simple and direct
- Emphasis is placed on:
  - component structure  
  - state management  
  - reusability  
  - clarity over cleverness  

Not everything here is experimental.  
Most of it is intentional repetition — tuning rather than invention.

---

## Philosophy

The race isn’t won in experiments alone.

It’s won in the garage —  
where systems are adjusted,  
mistakes are corrected,  
and performance is refined before returning to the track.

---

## Relationship to the Main Journey

- **Main chapters** document evolution and exploration  
- **Tuning Garage** documents discipline and execution  

Both are necessary.  
Only one is loud.

---

> Work done here doesn’t change the story.  
> It sharpens the driver.
