### Tuning Garage

This directory exists off the main track.

**Tuning Garage** is where real-world pressure enters — deadlines, constraints, requirements, and expectations.  
The work here is not about exploration or storytelling. It is about execution.

This is where skills are tested under practical conditions.

---

## What Happens Here

Work in **Tuning Garage** focuses on:

- React-based project assignments
- Internship deliverables and tasks
- Component-driven development
- State management and data flow
- UI logic under constraints
- Refactoring, fixes, and improvements

These projects are built to work — not to impress.

---

## Why This Exists

The main chapters document evolution.  
The garage documents discipline.

Not everything belongs in the storyline of chapters and incidents.  
Some work exists to sharpen instincts, improve speed, and build reliability.

That work lives here.

This is where repetition becomes mastery.

---

## How to Read This Directory

- Each subfolder represents a **focused phase of tuning**
- Naming is practical and direct
- Emphasis is placed on:
  - clarity over cleverness
  - structure over shortcuts
  - consistency over novelty

If something breaks here, it is fixed — not dramatized.

---

## Relationship to the Main Journey

- **Main Chapters** → exploration, progression, philosophy
- **Tuning Garage** → execution, adjustment, readiness

One builds vision.  
The other builds confidence.

Both are necessary.

---

## Philosophy

Speed is meaningless without control.

The race is won before the track —  
in the garage,  
where systems are tuned,  
mistakes are corrected,  
and performance is refined quietly.

---

> The work done here doesn’t change the story.  
> It sharpens the one telling it.
